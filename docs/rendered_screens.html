MOWR · UI direction v2

A monochrome utility marketplace, with green as a signal.

The new direction: MOWR should read like a fast, trusted service platform — Uber-simple, clean black-and-white, warm off-white grounds — not a gardening app. Green isn't decoration; it earns its place on one thing at a time: the primary action, a selected option, live progress, success, an available service. Thin borders instead of shadows; left-aligned headings; one clear action per screen.

Cards: 12px radius, 1px border, no shadow, 16px padding — and only when things genuinely belong together. Bottom nav is four items: Home · Bookings · Properties · Account.

Do & don't

  • Do assign green a role: primary action, selected, live, success, available.
  • Do left-align headings and use full-width content.
  • No floating card around every heading + button.
  • No icons inside coloured circles, or several icon sets.
  • No bright-green backgrounds, big gradients, or centred marketing heroes.
  • No everything pill-shaped — pills mean status only.

Customer

Clean home, one-question booking, clear status. Today MOWR does one thing — lawn mowing, with edging as an option — and does it well. The lawn personality lives in the name and photography, not the chrome.

Mower

The operator's day: their route, what a job does to it, and getting paid. Same restraint — data-forward, green for live/earned.

Admin

Operations at a glance. Dense, scannable, semantic colour — warning/critical stand out, everything else stays quiet.

Where this is applied: the new palette, Inter type scale, 12px radii, thin borders and left-aligned app bars are now in the Flutter theme (app_colors.dart / app_theme.dart). The screens above show the target; a widget-level pass is still rolling the old bright-green cards, icon-in-circle avatars and centred headings over to this system. Green stays a signal, not a coat of paint.
